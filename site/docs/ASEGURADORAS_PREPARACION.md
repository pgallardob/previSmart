# Three insurer preparation profiles, available without SCOMP

The advisor can prepare a comparison immediately using dated public product facts and insurer-obligation rating reports. No client SCOMP, company login or personalized quotation is required to view these profiles, produce a document or prepare comparable questions.

## Initial research set

| Insurer | Rating in the consulted report | Agency | Report date |
|---|---|---|---|
| BICE Vida | AA+, Stable | Moody's Local Chile | 2026-06-11 |
| Confuturo insurer (not its holding) | AA+, Stable | Feller Rate | 2026-01-08 |
| Consorcio Vida | AAA, Stable | Moody's Local Chile | 2026-09-10 |

The set is alphabetical and is not a ranking, recommendation or complete market inventory. A rating is a dated reference about insurance obligations, not a personal pension price or a guarantee of payment. Review updated official sources before making a formal decision.

## What the profiles contain

- Publicly described immediate, temporary/deferred and mixed annuity modalities.
- Published optional clauses, with company-specific documentation and observations.
- Source links, consultation date and the SHA-256 of each consulted rating report.
- Client offers matched conservatively by company name when later imported or entered manually.
- Costs described as **not individually published in the consulted product source**, never as free or zero. Supplied commissions remain separate from general public product information.
- Official product/report/simulator links, without automatic transmission or prefilling of client data.

In **Propuesta y ofertas**, the profiles appear even with no quotations. The client document contains a three-company preparation table; the technical appendix includes each complete profile and its citations. Actual quote amounts remain in their own comparison table, not invented from the public profiles or RP simulation.

## Source-quality controls

The Consorcio commercial page still displays AA+, while the consulted September 2026 insurer report hosted by CMF assigns AAA. The dated report is used and the discrepancy is disclosed. BICE Vida absorbed Vida Security on 2026-01-01; historical quote names are retained, not reassigned or counted as a fourth independent profile. Confuturo's insurer report is not confused with a rating of Inversiones Confuturo.

Some product pages retain outdated legal thresholds and historic benefits. These are not imported into PreviSmart's normative calculations. Public product features do not certify that a particular clause was included in a client's contract. Full copyrighted reports are not redistributed; only factual summaries, provenance and links are bundled.
